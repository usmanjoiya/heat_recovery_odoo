    Import/Export Functions for Sale Orders

    Odoo Quickbooks Online Connector facilitates you to easily import/export the sale orders. The module allows you to import as well as export the partners, categories, product, and sale order from Odoo to Quickbooks Online and vice versa.

    Import/Export Functions for Purchase Orders

    Odoo Quickbooks Online Connector facilitates you to easily import/export the Purchase orders. The module allows you to import as well as export the vendors, categories, product, and purchase order from Odoo to Quickbooks Online and vice versa.

    Import/Export Functions for Accounting

    Odoo Quickbooks Online Connector facilitates you to easily import/export the Accounting. The module allows you to import the Sale Taxes, Tax Codes, Payment Methods, Payment terms, Invoice, Payment, and Account from Quickbooks Online to Odoo. You can export accounts, invoice and payment from Odoo to Quickbooks Online.
